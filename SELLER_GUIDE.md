# 🎫 Sally Joy Camps - SELLER GUIDE

**For all staff selling tickets at different booths/locations**

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Open app on your phone**
   - Ask your manager for the link
   - Example: `http://192.168.1.10:5000` (on WiFi)
   - OR: `https://your-app-name.railway.app` (online)

2. **Sign In**
   - Enter your name: "John Banda"
   - Enter your location: "Main Gate Booth 1"
   - Tap **Sign In**
   - That's it! You're ready to sell.

---

## 💰 Selling a Ticket

### Step-by-Step

1. **Customer comes to your booth**
   - Customer decides which ticket: K50, K80, or K150
   - Customer pays you (cash, mobile money, bank transfer, WhatsApp)

2. **Verify payment**
   - Check they actually paid
   - Money is in your hand/account

3. **On your phone, tap 🎟️ Sell Ticket**

4. **Enter customer details:**
   - **Customer Name** → Full name (e.g., "Mary Simwaba")
   - **Phone Number** → Contact (e.g., "+260977123456")
   - **Ticket Type** → What they bought (K50, K80, or K150)
   - **How They Paid** → Cash / Mobile Money / Bank / WhatsApp
   - Tap **✨ Generate & Share Ticket**

5. **System generates QR code**
   - Beautiful QR code appears
   - Customer details shown

6. **Choose how to give ticket to customer** (pick ONE)

---

## 📱 Three Ways to Give Ticket to Customer

### **Option 1: WhatsApp (RECOMMENDED)**
- Easiest & fastest
- Tap **📱 Send WhatsApp** button
- Your phone opens WhatsApp
- Select customer from contacts
- Send the message (it has the link)
- Customer gets link instantly
- Customer taps link → sees their ticket
- Done! ✅

### **Option 2: Print QR Code**
- Tap **🖨️ Print** button
- Printer prints:
  - Customer's name
  - QR code
  - Ticket type & price
  - Event info
- Give printed ticket to customer
- Customer brings it to the gate

### **Option 3: Show on Phone**
- Tap **📲 Show Customer**
- Hold your phone to customer
- They screenshot the QR code
- They save to their phone
- Done! ✅

## ⚠️ Common Issues

### **"Camera won't open"**
- Use ⌨️ Manual Entry instead
- Ask customer for their ticket ID
- Type it in → Check in ✅

### **"App says ticket already checked in"**
- Someone already scanned it
- Happens when:
  - Customer checked in twice
  - Duplicate scan
- This is normal, just move to next customer

### **"I need to sign in again"**
- No problem!
- Close app
- Reopen
- Tap ⚙️ Settings
- Sign in with your name & location again

### **"Internet is slow/not working"**
- If on WiFi: Move closer to router
- If online app: Check internet connection
- If local server: Check with manager
- **You can still take payments** - ticket generates when internet is back

## 🛡️ IMPORTANT - Keep Secure

✅ **DO:**
- Keep the app link safe
- Don't share it with random people
- Close app when done selling
- Keep your location secure

❌ **DON'T:**
- Share login with other sellers (each person signs in separately)
- Give phone to customer to use the app (you control the app)
- Screenshot customer phone numbers
- Share customer data
